Splixy Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription at 2.99 EUR per month (the price may vary by country). Payment is charged to your store account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Questions

Does Splixy move money?

No. Splixy keeps track of who owes what and suggests how to settle up. Payments happen outside the app, however you prefer.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You can sign in with email, Google or Apple, so your groups are available on any device.

Can I use another currency?

Each group has its own currency, so you can keep a trip abroad separate from your everyday expenses.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by the store: on iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions; on Android open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the delete account page.
