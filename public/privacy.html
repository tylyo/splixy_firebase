Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Splixy app and website process, why, and what your rights are. Splixy helps you keep track of shared expenses with friends.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is tylyoDev, an independent developer. You can contact us at tylyodev@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

DataDetails
AccountEmail address, nickname, avatar and the sign-in method you choose (email and password, Google or Apple). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
Group contentGroup names, participants' names, expenses (name, amount, quantity, category or tags, date, notes), transfers, balances, the group's currency and who can view or edit it.
Other people's namesThe names you type, or choose from your contacts, to add participants to a group.
NotificationsA device token, only if you allow notifications, used to send you updates about your groups.
SubscriptionWhether you have Splixy Pro and until when. Payment is handled by Apple or Google: we never receive your card or payment details.
Account recordsTime of last sign-in, number of groups, whether your email is verified and when you accepted this policy and the Terms.
Technical dataThe services we use to run the app (see section 6) may process technical data such as IP address and device identifiers to deliver the service and keep it secure.

The app can ask for these permissions: camera, to scan a QR code to join a group (images are not stored); contacts, so you can pick participants by name (only the name you choose is used); photos, only if you choose a profile picture; notifications. You can change them at any time in your phone settings.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics services.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for automated decisions or profiling.

5. Who can see your data

  • The people you share a group with. They can see the group's content and participants' names and, depending on the permission you give, add, edit or delete expenses. Share a group only with people you trust.
  • Our service providers, listed below, who process data on our behalf.
  • Public authorities, only when required by law.

6. Service providers and transfers outside the EEA

Splixy runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging), provided by Google. Sign-in with Google or Apple and subscription payments are provided by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies.

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. Google relies on appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account we remove it as described in the delete account page, within 30 days. Data we must keep by law, and backups, are deleted when their retention period ends.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data (the export of a group in PDF or CSV is also available in the app), object to the processing, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to tylyodev@gmail.com; we will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Children

Splixy is not intended for people under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access rules that limit each group to its participants. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; for significant changes we will notify you in the app.
